\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{How to Sign In to Doenet for the First Time}}
\end{center}

\vspace{0.5em}

Doenet does not use traditional passwords. Instead, you will sign in using your school email account.


\section*{1. Open the Sign-In Screen}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.5em]
    \item \textbf{On a Phone (Mobile):} Go to \href{https://doenet.org}{doenet.org}. Tap the \textbf{menu icon (three horizontal lines)} at the top of your screen, then tap \textbf{Sign up/Log in} from the dropdown list. \textit{(Tip: You can also bypass the menu by going straight to \href{https://doenet.org/signIn}{doenet.org/signIn}.)}
    
    \item \textbf{On a Desktop or Laptop:} Go to \href{https://doenet.org}{doenet.org} and click the \textbf{Sign up/Log in} button directly in the top-right corner of the page.
    
    \item \textbf{On a Tablet (iPad, Android, Surface):} The layout depends on how you hold your screen. In \textbf{landscape (horizontal) mode}, you will usually see the \textbf{Sign up/Log in} button in the top corner just like a laptop. In \textbf{portrait (vertical) mode}, the narrower screen collapses the top bar into the \textbf{three-line menu icon} just like a phone.
\end{itemize}

\section*{2. Log In \& Set Up Your Profile}

Once you reach the sign-in screen, the steps are the same on every device:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.5em]
    \item \textbf{Enter your email:} Type your school email address into the box and select \textbf{Sign up/Log in}.
    
    \item \textbf{Check your inbox:} Check your inbox (and spam folder) for an email from \textbf{Doenet Accounts}, open the email, and click the blue \textbf{Complete Login} button.
    
    \item \textbf{Complete sign-up in:} This will take you back to the Doenet website where you will complete the sign-up process by entering your first and last name and clicking the blue \textbf{Submit} button.

    \item \textbf{You're done!} You will be taken to your Doenet profile page. Make sure you see your initials in a colored circle at the top of the page. You are now ready to start completing assignments.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Completing Assignments}

Make sure you are logged in to Doenet before clicking on any assignment links. If you click a link and see a page that says \textbf{Taking assignment as an anonymous user}, you are not logged in. If this happens, click the circle with the question mark and select \textbf{Sign in to save work}. After completing the sign-in process, return to the assignment link and click it again.
